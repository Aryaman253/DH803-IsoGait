Stand-still calibration

1. Place pods on the left and right foot with the pod logo facing outward.
2. Ask the participant to stand upright and still for 5 seconds.
3. Both pods record their resting angle, which becomes the zero line on the live charts.

Spare pod Ready

If a foot pod fails, flash the spare with that side's firmware (IsoGait_Left or IsoGait_Right). The hub identifies pods by their L / R id, so no change is needed here.
